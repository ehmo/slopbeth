# Code of Conduct

Keep project discussion useful and specific.

- Be direct without personal attacks.
- Critique outputs, rules, benchmarks, and claims.
- Do not post private text without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, or target people.
- Assume examples may be added to public benchmarks only when they are safe to publish.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close comments that make the project less useful or expose private material.
