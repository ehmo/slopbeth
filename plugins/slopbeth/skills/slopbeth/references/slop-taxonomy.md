# Slop taxonomy

Judge clusters rather than single tokens. A lone marker can be ordinary; repeated markers usually mean the prose is relying on machine-shaped defaults.

## Filler and windups

Common symptoms:

- throat-clearing before the claim
- "it is important to note" style framing
- summary paragraphs that restate the prompt
- transitions that signal structure without adding information

Rewrite target: start with the actual claim, detail, conflict, or consequence.

## Formulaic structures

Watch for:

- "not just X, but Y"
- "whether X or Y"
- "from X to Y"
- "at its core"
- "here's why" when the why is generic
- tidy three-item lists used as padding
- every paragraph ending in a miniature slogan

Rewrite target: keep the useful contrast, but make it concrete and asymmetric. If the contrast can fit any topic, it is probably filler.

## Abstract significance language

Signals:

- "underscores," "highlights," "showcases," "plays a crucial role"
- "transformative," "seamless," "robust," "dynamic," "comprehensive"
- "landscape," "ecosystem," "journey," "tapestry," "realm"
- claims of importance without stakes, mechanism, or evidence

Rewrite target: name the mechanism, cost, gain, failure mode, or tradeoff.

## Promotional inflation

Signals:

- benefits without constraints
- praise that could describe any product, person, or idea
- "unlock," "elevate," "empower," "supercharge," "game-changing"
- confident conclusions unsupported by the draft

Rewrite target: replace applause with evidence. If the product is useful, say what it lets someone do and what remains hard.

## False agency and actorless claims

Signals:

- "this ensures," "this allows," "it enables" with no actor
- passive-heavy sentences hiding who decided or did something
- abstractions acting like people

Rewrite target: put the actor and action back in the sentence unless the actor is genuinely unknown or irrelevant.

## Padding by range or balance

Signals:

- "from beginners to experts"
- "whether you're a founder, marketer, or creator"
- fake inclusiveness that adds no segmentation
- hedge stacks that avoid committing to a claim

Rewrite target: name the actual audience or delete the range.

## Formatting tells

Signals:

- Title Case headings for ordinary sections
- bold labels on every list item
- decorative emoji
- polished bullets with identical syntax
- concluding "in conclusion" or "ultimately" paragraphs
- citation and markup leftovers such as `turn0search0`, `contentReference`, `oaicite`, `oai_citation`, `utm_source=`, or placeholder URLs

Rewrite target: use structure only where it improves scanning. Do not decorate weak content or leave tool artifacts in the prose.

## Bland-clean failure

A rewrite can pass phrase lint and still fail. Look for sentences that can be moved to another topic with only noun swaps. Those sentences need a fact, scene, number, decision, contradiction, or consequence.
